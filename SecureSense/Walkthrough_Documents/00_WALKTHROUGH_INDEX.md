1. **01_QUICK_START.md** - one-command startup and service checklist.
2. **02_GOPHISH_SETUP_AND_CAMPAIGN.md** - one-time Gophish setup, sending profile, template, landing page, group and campaign creation.
3. **03_END_TO_END_practice.md** - presentation order from email delivery through learning and GRC evidence.
4. **04_TROUBLESHOOTING.md** - port conflicts, Gophish, certificate, synchronization, AI and login issues.
5. **05_practice_ACCOUNTS.md** - fictional exercise users.
6. **06_VIVA_CAMPAIGN_SHOWCASE.md** - five-category Gophish population, colorful email scenarios and viva practice order.
7. **SecureSense_Walkthrough.md** - formatted walkthrough for submission/review.

The current default local ports are SecureSense 8000, inbox 8025, sandbox SMTP 1028, SMTP proxy 1027, Gophish admin 3333 and Gophish phishing server 80 unless you deliberately configure an alternative.
